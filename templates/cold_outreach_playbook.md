## 2. Cold Outreach Strategy: Why SMS Beats Cold Email 10x

Contractor owners are rarely sitting behind a laptop. They are driving between jobs, inspecting roofs, or talking to supply houses. **Cold SMS has a 94% open rate and a 38% response rate with trade owners.**

### Step 1: The Curiosity SMS (Sent from a local US phone number via Twilio)
> *"Hey {{owner_first_name}}, saw your {{google_rating}}-star reviews in {{city}}. Quick question: are your sales reps still writing estimates on paper or taking 2 days to email PDFs after inspecting the roof?"*

### Step 2: The Response & Demo Hook (When they reply "Who is this?" or "We use Jobber/Paper")
> *"Hey {{owner_first_name}}, I built ApexRoof OS with a couple local Austin roofers. It lets your guys generate Good / Better / Best quotes with pitch & square calculations right on their phone, get the homeowner to e-sign in the driveway, and tap Apple Pay for the 30% deposit on the spot.*
>
> *I recorded a 60-second video of how it looks on an iPad: [loom.com/share/demo-link].*
>
> *Can I set up a free login for your crew to try on your next 3 jobs this week? Zero card needed."*
